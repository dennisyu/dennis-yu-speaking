# AI for Funeral Homes

**Format:** Keynote or workshop
**Proof:** NFDA Annual Convention keynote, “Embracing AI Technology: Practical Tools and Strategies for Funeral Directors”; NFDA podcast; deathcare workshops using Dollar a Day

## Description

A category most agencies ignore. Dennis shows funeral directors how AI helps the firm show up with dignity, respond to families faster, and own local search — then proves it live on member sites.

At NFDA he took randomly selected members through simple AI steps (website, video, ads) and revealed results in the room, plus the pitfalls: hallucinations, false information, accidental plagiarism, SEO penalties.

## Designed for

National Funeral Directors Association, state funeral associations, deathcare marketing groups, cemetery and cremation operators.
