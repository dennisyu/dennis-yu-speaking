# AI Keynote for Your Industry

**Format:** 45–60 minutes, main stage
**Best for:** association annual meetings, trade shows, entrepreneur summits
**Fee:** $5,000 U.S. / $7,500 international + travel

## Description

Most AI keynotes are a tour of tools. This one is a live build in your category.

Dennis Yu, CEO of Local Service Spotlight, takes a real business from the room — a funeral home, a landscaper, a contractor, an affiliate offer, a campus program — and deploys AI agents on stage: search, content, ads, and follow-up. The Content Factory and Dollar a Day frameworks sit underneath, so attendees leave with a repeatable system, not a list of apps.

He has run this for NFDA funeral directors, volleyball club operators, home-service owners, affiliates in Dubai, and marketers at DigiMarCon, PubCon, and Social Media Marketing World.

## Takeaways

- Watch AI do the work on a business that looks like yours
- Leave with the 4-stage Content Factory sequence
- Know which tasks to hand to agents vs. keep with humans
- Avoid the usual AI traps: hallucinations, plagiarism, SEO penalties
