# Live Main-Stage Audit

**Format:** 30–90 minutes, keynote or breakout
**Best for:** rooms that are tired of theory

## Description

Dennis audits a real member business in real time — website, maps, reviews, ads, AI visibility — with agents running on screen. The Better Business Bureau booked him to train accredited owners; the hour ran 88 minutes and the participant count did not drop until the end.

Use this when the organizer wants the audience to come up afterward asking to implement, not asking for the slide deck.
