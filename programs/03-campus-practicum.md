# Campus Practicum: AI Builders for Colleges

**Format:** Guest lecture, multi-day intensive, or semester partnership
**Best for:** marketing, business, journalism, and continuing-ed programs
**Fee:** from $5,000 U.S. / $7,500 international + travel; semester partnerships quoted

## Description

A higher-level practicum in live AI implementation. Students bring laptops, follow proven SOPs, and deploy agents on real partner businesses — plumbers, clubs, local services — so the portfolio is real work, not a case study.

Johns Hopkins ran Applied Digital Marketing: Collaborations with Local Businesses with Dennis as instructor (six sessions, spring 2025). Gonzaga and other campuses have booked guest sessions. The standing offer is a pipeline into the AI Builder apprenticeship track.

## Takeaways

- Hands-on curriculum, not lecture-and-test
- Real businesses and real outcomes
- A path from classroom to paid AI Builder work
