# Short bio (online / 800–1,200 characters)

Dennis Yu is CEO of Local Service Spotlight. He delivers AI keynotes and hands-on AI agent workshops for industry associations, universities, and conferences — including funeral homes, landscapers, entrepreneurs, affiliates, and digital marketers.

Most speakers show slides. Dennis shows up with a live client, a laptop, and an AI agent he builds in front of the room. Audiences watch the Content Factory and Dollar a Day frameworks turn into working systems: local search, content, ads, and follow-up that keep running after the session.

He is a former Yahoo search engineer who has managed more than a billion dollars in Google and Facebook advertising for brands including Nike, Red Bull, Rosetta Stone, and the Golden State Warriors. He co-authored *Facebook Nation*, a textbook used in more than 700 colleges, and has delivered 1,000+ talks across 17 countries on 5 continents.

His mission is creating one million digital marketing jobs by certifying young adults as AI Builders and pairing them with local service businesses. He previously operated under BlitzMetrics.
